# Dylan Gallez | Materials & Systems Scientist

---

### Technical Focus
Interdisciplinary scientist and systems engineer with expertise in materials science, automated sensing, mechatronics, and distributed control systems. Driven by a passion for understanding how chemistry and physics govern material performance, I focus on bridging the gap between physical properties and real-world systems and execution. My core interests lie at the intersection of automation and nanotechnology, integrating sensing and control systems with nanoscale processes and machinery for advanced hardware applications. I bring the technical expertise to assure quality, improve reliability, and overcome manufacturing challenges in aerospace, electronics, and other high-tech industries.

* **Mechatronics & Hardware Systems:** Microcontrollers and single-board computers (ESP32, Raspberry Pi), electromechanical, fluidic, and vacuum systems, CAD/CAM prototyping (Autodesk Fusion 360), 3D printing (FDM), hardware troubleshooting
* **Software & IoT Architecture:** Microservice orchestration / messaging via Docker & NATS, bare-metal scripting (MicroPython), PostgreSQL/SQLite ledgers and relational databases, and Python data & orchestration pipelines (Pydantic, Pandas, Matplotlib)
* **Materials Science & Microfabrication:** Thin-film deposition (CVD, electroplating, electroforming), materials characterization & failure analysis (FIB-SEM, Optical Profilometry, load frames), substrate preparation (wet chemical cleaning & etching, sputtering), and regulated laboratory execution (cGMP, GLP, GDP, ISO 6 cleanroom)
